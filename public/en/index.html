How it works

Four steps from problem to solution.

  1. 1

    Write

    Pick the company and describe what happened, with dates and details.

  2. 2

    Moderation

    Moderators review the complaint before it is published.

  3. 3

    Reply

    The company is notified and answers publicly on the same page.

  4. 4

    Resolution

    You mark it as resolved or keep talking to the company.

For consumers

Publish your complaint for free, follow the company's reply and read other people's experiences before you buy or sign.

For companies

Be the first to answer customers, fix problems and show the public how you handle complaints.

Publishing rules

To keep complaints useful and fair.

Facts only

Write what happened. Complaints with insults or baseless accusations are not published.

No personal data

Do not post anyone's phone number, address or ID documents.

Right of reply

Every company can respond, and the reply appears next to the complaint.
